Get to Know Me
THE GIRL Behind the Content

I'm Justine, a DMV/NY content creator currently based in Accra, Ghana. I started sharing my travel experiences after discovering an early love for learning about different cultures while growing up in NYC's large multicultural community.

I'm all about sharing one-of-a-kind experiences in different cities, highlighting amazing restaurants and cultural dishes, and providing tips for navigating new places. I believe in community building, authenticity, and creativity.

I've visited 33+ countries and lived in four — Belgium, Malaysia, Sudan, and now Ghana.

Are you a travel brand with an exciting campaign? A tourism board looking to make your destination a must-visit? Let's create impactful, results-driven content together.
